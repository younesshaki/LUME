# Page 1 — "The Problem"

## Prompt
Manga-style comic page, 3 panels. Warm neutral tones. Panel 1: A young developer with glasses in a blue hoodie sits at a messy computer desk. The desktop on their laptop screen shows files named "report_v3.py", "report_final.py", "report_actually_final_v2.py" — they look frustrated. Panel 2: A small orange/red floating cube mascot appears beside them saying "There's a better way" in a speech bubble. Panel 3: The mascot opens a magical glowing door revealing a beautiful timeline of snapshot boxes (commits) each with a label. Clean white backgrounds, manga art style.

## Characters
- **Dev**: Young developer, glasses, blue hoodie, short dark hair, frustrated then curious
- **Git Mascot**: Small floating orange/red cube, friendly and knowledgeable
- **Setting**: Messy desk → magical timeline reveal

## Style
- Art: manga
- Tone: neutral
- Layout: standard (3 panels)
- Aspect: portrait (3:4)
