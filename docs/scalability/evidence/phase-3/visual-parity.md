All captures use Chromium, reduced motion and identical public data. No live domain was redeployed. The before application is `cff045f`; the after application is `458996a`. The staging tenant remains demo; Glo3D/light/dark are presentation overrides.

## Live Vite extraction: full chrome and behavior

Production builds retain the actual live `VITE_PAGE_RENDERER=false` setting. The top/bottom viewport captures include original navigation, footer, dock and vehicle detail. Each case also compares document title/text, consent and applicable product filters/detail/back, contact submission, gallery and inquiry dialogs. The contact submission is a transport fixture; hosted real writes are recorded separately.

The raw PNG comparator uses Playwright pixelmatch threshold **0.1**, **zero permitted different pixels** after perceptual antialiasing treatment; no masks. All 40 comparisons pass. Raw RGB noise counts and deltas are retained in [the report](live-vite/report.json); [raw capture hashes](live-vite/capture-hashes.json) refer to PNGs before JPEG review encoding. No intentional Vite visual or behavioral changes.
