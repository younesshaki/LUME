# LUME Brand Direction

## Project Purpose

LUME is a fake premium luxury brand website.

This project is not a generic portfolio site and not a mass-market ecommerce
experience. It should present LUME as a high-end luxury brand that reimagines
regular everyday products as premium statement objects.

## Core Brand Idea

LUME creates luxury versions of familiar products.

Example:

- a luxury version of Red Bull
- black and gold packaging
- elevated materials, finish, and presentation
- cinematic, aspirational, premium positioning

The brand should feel exclusive, polished, and intentionally excessive in a
controlled way.

## Creative Direction

Anyone working on this repo should preserve this direction:

- premium luxury, not playful consumer tech
- bold, cinematic presentation
- restrained, confident copy
- high-contrast visual identity
- rich material language: black, gold, metallic, gloss, shadow, reflection
- product-first storytelling
- fewer elements, stronger composition

The experience should feel like a luxury campaign microsite or premium concept
launch, not a normal storefront.

## Product Framing

The products are fictional luxury reinterpretations of regular products.

That means:

- the products can be inspired by real everyday categories
- the branding, packaging, and presentation should feel original
- the goal is desirability and visual storytelling, not realism or utility alone

## UX Expectations

The site should prioritize:

- cinematic reveal of the hero product
- strong product imagery or video
- elegant transitions between scenes
- minimal but deliberate text
- premium pacing
- clear hierarchy between hero moment, product detail, and next action

Avoid:

- cluttered ecommerce layouts
- generic SaaS patterns
- playful startup language
- busy dashboards
- overly technical UI framing

## Guidance For Future Devs And AI Agents

When making decisions, optimize for brand perception first.

If a choice is technically correct but visually cheapens the experience, it is
the wrong choice for this project.

Before adding or changing anything, ask:

1. Does this make LUME feel more premium?
2. Does this support a luxury brand narrative?
3. Does this make the product look more desirable?
4. Does this preserve a cinematic, high-end presentation?

If the answer is no, revise the approach.
