# LUME — Brand Document

---

## The Concept

LUME is a secret luxury hotel in Monaco. It is not a place you find — it is a place you are invited to.

What makes LUME unlike anything else is its founding philosophy: access is not granted by wealth, it is granted by impact. The guests of LUME are people who have positively influenced society and helped others. That is the only currency that gets you through the door.

Once invited, you are placed on the list. From that moment, you can request a stay whenever availability allows — but only once per year. When your annual visit ends, your card resets. You wait. You think about it. You come back.

---

## The Hotel

- **Location:** Monaco — chosen for its association with extreme discretion, luxury, and inaccessibility
- **Size:** ~70 rooms and suites
- **Access:** Invitation-only. One stay per year per guest.
- **Design:** Dark and gold. An architecture and interior design never seen before — every detail screams luxury and class
- **Atmosphere:** The kind of place you keep thinking about until your next stay

---

## The Philosophy of Products

LUME collaborates with the biggest brands on earth to offer special edition products that exist **nowhere else in the world**.

These are not products you can buy. They are not products you can take home. They exist only within LUME — you experience them during your stay, and when you leave, they stay behind. This is intentional. It makes the stay itself the product.

Every item in LUME is a direct collab — the brand creates a version specifically for LUME, and LUME is the sole place it exists.

## Suggested Products to Develop

These follow the same pattern — biggest brand in their category, exclusive edition, never for sale.

**Food & Drink**
- LUME x Moët & Chandon — exclusive champagne blend, served only at LUME
- LUME x Evian — limited crystal bottle, room water experience

**Fashion**
- LUME x Hermès — an exclusive piece (scarf, belt, or small leather good) guests can wear during their stay
- LUME x Rolex — a watch you wear inside LUME. It stays when you leave.

**Tech**
- LUME x Bang & Olufsen — the in-room audio system, exclusive hardware never sold anywhere
- LUME x Leica — an exclusive camera edition available to use during the stay, left behind at checkout

**Wellness & Experiences**
- LUME Gym — equipped exclusively with LUME x Technogym machines, a line that doesn't exist outside
- LUME x a Michelin-starred chef — a private dining experience, menu changes with every guest
- LUME x Ferrari or Lamborghini — a private driving experience in Monaco, exclusive to guests

**Art**
- LUME x [rotating artist] — a permanent installation that changes annually, commissioned exclusively for LUME

## Website Structure

Three sections. Intentionally minimal.

```
/                   → Brand entry — dark, cinematic, brand statement
/products           → Browsable product grid (modern, editorial)
/products/[slug]    → Individual product cinematic experience (like the Red Bull showcase)
/story              → The story of LUME — philosophy, origin, what it stands for
/access             → Get Access — invitation request form
```

**Key UX principle:** The product grid is browsable, but each product page is its own cinematic world — the Red Bull showcase is the template for how every product gets its own experience.
